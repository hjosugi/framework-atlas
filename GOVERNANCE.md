# Governance

Framework Atlas uses evidence-first maintenance.

- A framework description may be merged when its scope and source are clear.
- A lineage edge requires an explicit source or must remain marked as a hypothesis.
- Popularity does not determine inclusion; technical distinctiveness and historical relevance matter.
- Archived and discontinued frameworks remain because failed approaches and transitions are part of the design record.
- Maintainer statements and official design documents take priority over secondary summaries.
- Conflicting evidence is preserved instead of silently resolved.

The initial owner acts as editor. As the project grows, maintainers may be assigned by language or domain.
