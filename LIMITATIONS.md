# Limitations

A literal, permanently complete list of every framework cannot be proven from one GitHub topic. Topic labels are user-assigned, repositories may be private or hosted elsewhere, names collide, projects are renamed or archived, and search results change continuously.

Framework Atlas therefore treats completeness as an observable process:

1. Preserve dated raw snapshots.
2. Separate curated records from unreviewed candidates.
3. Report coverage by language and domain.
4. Generate issues for missing evidence and unclassified candidates.
5. Never convert similarity into a historical influence claim without evidence.

The initial release is a broad research foundation, not a claim that no framework exists outside the dataset.
